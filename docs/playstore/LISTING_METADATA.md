1. **⚡ Sub-100ms Biometric Recognition**
   Experience unmatched recognition speed powered by on-device AI and Qualcomm Hexagon NPU optimization. No waiting in queues; scan and verify up to 60 students or employees per minute.

2. **🔒 100% Offline-First Architecture**
   No internet? Zero problem. OmniFace AI performs all face detection, 3D anti-spoofing liveness checks, and template matching locally inside your device’s hardware-backed cryptographic Keystore.

3. **🛡️ DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR Privacy Guarantee**
   We value your privacy and compliance above all. **OmniFace AI NEVER stores, uploads, or transmits raw face photos or video footage.** Camera frames are processed ephemerally in volatile RAM and converted into 512-dimensional mathematical vectors. Raw images are instantly wiped.

4. **👁️ 3D Liveness & Anti-Spoofing Defense**
   Equipped with deep 3D facial geometry reconstruction and multi-angle verification to proactively block printed photos, screen recordings, cut-out masks, and video replays.

5. **🧾 Aegis Blockchain Tamper-Proof Ledger**
   Every check-in is cryptographically hashed with SHA-256 and anchored in a local tamper-evident blockchain ledger. Records cannot be retroactively altered, manipulated, or forged.

6. **📊 Instant Excel & PDF Reports**
   Generate audit-ready monthly registers, daily check-in logs, and absent summaries with one tap. Share directly via WhatsApp, Email, or Google Drive.

7. **📱 Two-Factor 2D/3D Mode (Optional)**
   Pair student ID cards or QR/barcodes with facial biometrics for bulletproof two-factor identity verification in examinations and high-security zones.
