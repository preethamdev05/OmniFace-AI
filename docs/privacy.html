Official Google Play Privacy Policy

OmniFace AI — Privacy & Biometric Data Governance

Package Name: com.omniface.ai • Effective Date: September 3, 2026 • Governing Frameworks: Google Play Data Safety, DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, CCPA, FERPA

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: 100% On-Device Processing

OmniFace AI processes all biometric facial recognition locally on your hardware. Raw camera images and mathematical facial feature templates are NEVER transmitted to developer servers, advertising networks, or analytics brokers. Your biometrics remain strictly sealed inside your device's hardware-encrypted enclave.

1. Categories of Data Processed

A. Biometric Data (100% Local)

  • 512-Dimensional Vector Embeddings: Deep neural feature abstractions (MobileFaceNet / ArcFace). These normalized numerical unit vectors cannot be reverse-engineered into photographs.
  • Facial Landmark Geometry: 5 canonical fiducials and 468-point 3D topological mesh coordinates used in volatile RAM exclusively for orientation and Presentation Attack Detection (PAD).
  • Storage & Cryptography: Biometric templates are stored in an on-device SQLite database encrypted with AES-256-GCM using master keys sealed within the hardware AndroidKeyStore.

B. Contactless rPPG Pulse Telemetry (Vitality & Anti-Spoofing)

  • Technical Function: Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) extracts capillary hemoglobin light absorption frequencies (0.75 Hz – 3.0 Hz, 45 – 180 BPM) using the Chrominance (CHROM) algorithm.
  • Security Purpose: Presentation Attack Detection (PAD). Live human skin demonstrates microscopic capillary pulse pulsatility; printed photos and screen replays do not.
  • Non-Medical Notice: This feature is solely an anti-spoofing defense and is NOT intended, certified, or used as a medical device or health diagnostic instrument. Pulse readings exist in volatile RAM and are never recorded to disk.

C. Academic & Attendance Records

  • Institutional Identifiers: Student Full Name, Roll Number, Department, and Semester.
  • Attendance Ledger: Session date, timestamp, match confidence score, and deterministic SHA-256 Aegis blockchain Merkle root block hashes.

2. User-Owned Google Drive Backups

OmniFace AI supports optional cloud backups owned 100% by the institutional administrator:

  • Direct Storage: Backups are stored directly in the administrator's personal Google Drive appDataFolder. Developer servers do not host, broker, or see the data.
  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Backups are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM with a key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (10,000 iterations) from a custom administrator PIN before upload.
  • Neither Google nor OmniFace can decrypt or view the biometric templates without the administrator's PIN.

5. Right-to-Forget & Data Deletion Instructions

OmniFace AI complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR Article 17:

  1. Single Profile Deletion: In the Student Directory, swipe left on any student record and tap Delete Biometric Profile to irreversibly wipe their vector embedding.
  2. Automated 90-Day Purge: Historical attendance logs older than 90 days are automatically pruned from the local database.
  3. Complete Master Wipe: Open Settings → Data Governance and tap Wipe All Biometric Data to perform a zero-fill cryptographic erase of all biometric templates and ledger records.
